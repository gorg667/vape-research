# Research notes 02 — Canadian disposable market overview (2026)

## Key market shift (2025–2026)
- Market has moved to ULTRA-high capacity "disposables": 25–30 mL tanks, 50K–80K advertised puffs, 850–1000 mAh
  rechargeable batteries (USB-C), LED/HD screens showing battery + e-liquid %, multiple power modes (Eco/Normal/Boost/Pulse),
  adjustable airflow, dual-mesh coils. Prices $40–53 CAD in Ontario.
- Mid tier: 15–20 mL, 20K–30K puffs, $25–36.
- Small/compact: 2–8 mL, 800–8000 puffs, $12–25. (Allo Ultra 2500, Vice Twist, Vuse Go.)
- Hybrid "pod + reusable battery" systems eat into disposables: Flavour Beast Level X (Boost G2 Ultra), STLTH Loop / Loop Max,
  Beast Mode pods compatible with Level X battery. Lower cost per mL because you re-use the battery.

## CRITICAL insight from users (reddit r/electronic_cigarette 2025): PUFF COUNT IS FICTION — buy by mL
- "70K puffs" Vice Box 2 = 30 mL. Heavy user finished in 3 days. Commenters: 30 mL ≈ 3–7 days for heavy vaper; ~1 week+ for moderate.
- Puff counts are calculated at ~1.8–2 s per puff at lowest power mode. Real draws are longer; Boost/Pulse modes burn 25–40% faster.
- Rule of thumb: compare devices by **mL of e-liquid** and **price per mL** — not puffs. Also battery mAh matters for how often you recharge (not longevity).
- At 20 mg/mL, 30 mL = 600 mg nicotine total. (A cigarette delivers ~1–2 mg absorbed.) Heavy vapers vastly out-consume smokers.

## Reliability complaints (reddit r/disposablevapecanada Nov 2025)
- Beast Mode Max 2 DOA (screen lights, no vapour). Store refused refund -> "contact manufacturer". Manufacturer DID replace by email.
- Clutch Vape (GTA chain) order: 2 of 3 devices leaked in packaging; 10 days to get credit.
- Vice: prior defect reports; older Vice Box "tastes like poison when charging" (2022).
- Advice: **test the device in-store before leaving**; keep receipt + box (excise stamp, batch) for manufacturer warranty claims.
- Disposables: essentially no recourse for duds except manufacturer goodwill.

## Bias note
All "best of" lists found so far are retailer blogs -> ranking = what they stock/margin. Use them for SPECS + PRICES, not verdicts.
Our verdicts should weight: mL capacity, $/mL, reliability reports, brand track record, flavour consensus across multiple sources, features.
